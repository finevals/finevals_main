Regulatory-Grade AI Evaluations for Financial Services Companies

Your engineering team's evals tell you the AI works. They will not survive an audit. FinEvals produces evidence that a second-line validator, internal audit and a regulator will accept — and says plainly what it cannot cover.

Our Solution — Turning AI governance, risk, and compliance into executable evaluations.

Eval Packs

Scenarios built with a known ground truth, so a wrong answer is provable rather than arguable. Each pack states what it tests — and what it cannot.

Deterministic Checks

Most grading is arithmetic against a ground truth we constructed. No model, no rubric, no validation study — because there is nothing to validate.

Validated Judges

Where judgement is unavoidable, each judge ships with its agreement study — including how closely human experts agree with each other.

Audit Evidence

A signed, versioned evidence pack. Every result traces to the run that produced it, and the assessment can be reproduced a year later.

How It Works

Second-line validation teams are asked to sign off on AI systems using evidence they cannot defend. We give them evidence that holds.

1. Select an eval pack — AML investigation support, credit decisioning, customer communications.
2. Runs in your own cloud: eval datasets and grading logic are deployed to your environment.
3. Generate evidence: a signed report, mapped to the obligations it evidences, produced automatically.

No SaaS lock-in. No data egress. No security review nightmare.
